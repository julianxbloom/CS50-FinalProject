What is Trust Score?

Trust Score is what keeps Debate.'s environment healthy. It reflects how trusful of a debater you are. Lower it gets, heavier will be the consequences.
By default, you own 75 points.
If your score got lower, you can regain trust if you behave well for a certain period of time.
